===============================================================================
  LOW BLOW  //  KICK / BASS DUCKER  //  Castle Danger Music Company
===============================================================================

Kick and bass fight over the same low end. Low Blow goes on the BASS track and
listens to the KICK on its sidechain input: every kick hit pulls the bass's low
end down for a moment, so the kick punches through and the bass fills the gaps.


SETTING IT UP
-------------
1. Put Low Blow on your bass track (after the amp sim / EQ, before any bus).
2. Send the kick to Low Blow's sidechain input:
     Fender Studio Pro: open Low Blow's window, click the sidechain button in
       the plugin header and pick your kick track (or add a send on the kick
       channel and choose "Low Blow" as the destination).
     Logic: the Side Chain menu at the top of the plugin window.
     Ableton, Reaper, Cubase: route the kick to the plugin's sidechain input.
   On a drum bus or a stereo kick, either works.
3. KICK ON THE SIDECHAIN lights up green with the number of hits a minute.
   Until then Low Blow leaves the bass alone.


THE RING (the scope, last 4 seconds)
------------------------------------
  Grey ......... the bass, as it comes out
  Red line ..... the kick on the sidechain; red ticks = hits
  Yellow ....... how far the bass is ducked, hanging from the top
                 (dashed line = DEPTH)


THE HIT
-------
  DEPTH ........ how far the bass goes down on each kick (0-24 dB).
                 4-8 dB clears room; 10+ gives a pumping effect.
  ATTACK ....... how fast it gets out of the way (0.1-20 ms). Short.
  HOLD ......... how long it stays down after the kick fades (0-150 ms)
  RELEASE ...... how fast it comes back (10-500 ms). Short for fast double
                 kicks, longer for a smooth or pumping feel.
  SENSITIVITY .. which hits count. The trigger follows your loudest recent
                 kicks, so you never set a threshold: higher also catches
                 soft hits and ghost notes, lower only the big ones.

WHERE
-----
  LOWS ONLY .... ducks only the bass below CROSSOVER, so its growl, pick
                 attack and upper notes stay put (recommended)
  FULL ......... ducks the whole bass
  CROSSOVER .... 40-300 Hz. Just above the kick's thump: 80-150 Hz is usual.
  LOOKAHEAD .... 0-5 ms. Starts the duck a hair before the kick lands. Adds
                 the same latency; your DAW compensates.
  OUTPUT ....... -12 to +12 dB

METERS
------
  KICK ......... sidechain level; the label lights on every hit
  DUCK ......... how far the bass is down right now (white line = peak,
                 grey line = DEPTH)
  LISTEN ....... hear only what Low Blow is taking out of the bass. Turn it
                 off before you bounce. (Never saved in a preset.)


WITH THE MASTER
---------------
Low Blow talks to The Master over Castle Danger Link. With The Servant on your
kick and bass (ROLE Kick and Bass), The Master works out how much they fight
in the low end and, at APPLY, sets Low Blow's DEPTH (from the genre and how
much they overlap), CROSSOVER (above where the kick's thump sits) and RELEASE
(from the gap between your kick hits, so it's back before the next one).
Without Low Blow, The Master suggests it when kick and bass collide. If the
sidechain isn't connected, The Master says so in its log.
